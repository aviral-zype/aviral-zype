# Hi, I'm Aviral 👋

Full-stack engineer with **6+ years of experience**, working on **OTT / video streaming apps** across web, mobile, TV and the backends behind them. I started out frontend-heavy (React, JavaScript) and now ship end-to-end: from the API and catalog services to the apps people watch on.

## What I work on

- **Streaming apps on every screen:** web (React), iOS & tvOS, Android & Android TV, Roku, and Smart TVs (Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Xbox).
- **Backend & APIs:** Ruby on Rails services, GraphQL APIs, background jobs, and the feeds that drive the apps.
- **Video playback & monetization:** player integrations (Video.js, Bitmovin), VAST ad scheduling, TVOD purchases, entitlements and TV-provider sign-in.
- **Build & release:** CI/CD for app builds (Xcode Cloud, CircleCI) and app-store signing.
- **Security:** locking down storage and secrets on AWS (private buckets, signed links, KMS-backed secrets) without taking live services down.
